# Peach Health

> **As built, 25 Oct 2026.** A small, UI-focused case made from the Peach Health section of Oleh's Figma portfolio file. It has no handoff MD. The slide and case study are in `src/content/project-slides.ts`, `src/content/case-studies.ts` and `src/content/peach-health-media.ts`, with images in `public/cases/peach-health/`. Peach Health is featured under Mobile after Nearbor, with the Heartbeat icon. It replaces the hidden "Peach Health" placeholder in More projects. Confirmed by Oleh (25 Oct): a personal concept, with Oleh as Product Designer; the overview, intro and My role say so.

## Request

"Small case study, but include all screens; 2 blocks in the overview, all others in the case study. More about UI."

## Overview (two blocks)

1. **Row:** Home, Health agents, Medication kit.
2. **Tabs:** Mood check, Pill taken?, Inhaler taken?, Lock-screen reminder.
