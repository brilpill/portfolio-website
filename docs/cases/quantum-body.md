## 3. Case study

Title: From a moment of reflection to a journal you can return to.
Intro: Quantum Body is designed around five ways to record a personal experience. Someone might arrive with a dream to remember, something they feel grateful for, or an intention they want to keep in view. The interface gives these practices a shared structure, with distinct symbols and colours, and brings entries together in the Alignment Log.
Hero: device · frame: row — new-entry.png "Choose a practice", manifestation-form.png "Create an entry", alignment-log.png "Return to the journal"

Chapter — Wireframes · Mapping the experience before the visual detail
Text: The wireframes cover the path from introducing the practices and setting reminders to writing and revisiting entries. They also include editing, archive, trash, and education screens. The gallery keeps these connected screens together so the underlying structure can be explored before looking at the final interface.

Chapter — Practices · Five starting points, a familiar form
