# SellerGenius

> **As built, 25 Oct 2026.** A small case from Oleh's short brief (below) plus the SellerGenius Figma file. The slide and case study are in `src/content/project-slides.ts`, `src/content/case-studies.ts` and `src/content/sellergenius-media.ts`, with images in `public/cases/sellergenius/`. It is featured under Web after AskFlow, with the ChartLineUp icon. All screens are sample. The copy describes visible design decisions only, and no outcomes are claimed.

## Brief (from Oleh)

SellerGenius: Web App
A 0>1 analytics platform for Amazon sellers and agencies, bringing financial metrics, advertising performance, and operational data into one dashboard for clearer profitability tracking and multi-brand management.

- My role: Product Designer / UX&UI / Design System / Branding
- Timeline: 3–4 mo · 2025
- Industry: E-commerce / Analytics SaaS

## Overview (two blocks)

1. A browser (bare) with tabs: Dashboard, Profit, Advertising, All brands, Ask AI.
2. A compare (bare): the dashboard in the light theme vs the dark theme.

## Case study

- **Hero:** the dashboard.
- **Dashboard:** customisation, through the edit grid, the widget library and the chart-view choice.
- **Profit and ads:** profit, P&L, profitability by SKU and fees; then advertising and recommendations.
- **Brands:** the multi-brand overview and the detail view for one brand.
- **AI:** the full-screen chat, a report built from a prompt, and the assistant window. These are labelled as Step 2 (post-MVP), as in the brief.
- **Themes:** the light vs dark compare, with one line on the brand identity and design system. The logo boards were removed on request (25 Oct).
- **Year and duration:** 2025, 3–4 months.
